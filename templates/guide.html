How to Use UpClip Studio

A step-by-step walkthrough of AI clipping, caption styling, and export workflows.

Guide Progress Step 1 of 10

1. Upload Source Video

Drag and drop or browse to upload any video file (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM). Files are processed locally on your device with high performance.

2. Language & Subtitles

Whisper AI transcribes spoken audio into word-level timestamps. You can auto-detect the spoken language or translate subtitles into over 20 languages.

3. Aspect Ratio Reframing

Choose between 9:16 vertical (Shorts / Reels / TikTok), 16:9 landscape, or 1:1 square. Smart center-crop prevents stretching and maintains aspect integrity.

4. Scene Detection & Splitting

PySceneDetect scans visual boundaries and isolates engaging moments. You can let AI decide or define custom clip durations and targets.

5. Animated Karaoke Captions

Enable short-form animated karaoke subtitles. Active words highlight dynamically in sync with speech using FFmpeg ASS rendering.

6. Review & Edit

Preview each generated clip in the review grid. Rename clips, delete unwanted cuts, or transfer any clip directly into Caption Studio.

7. Caption Studio Timeline

Open Caption Studio for a full timeline editor. Drag caption timestamps, split lines, tweak fonts, active colors, and positioning.

8. Export & Quality Selection

Download all clips in a single ZIP archive, re-encode to 1080p/720p/360p, or download raw SRT/VTT subtitle files and JSON transcripts.

9. YouTube Downloader

Download long YouTube videos, playlists, or audio tracks directly into your local library for rapid clipping.

10. YouTube Automation Desk

Connect your YouTube channel, prepare titles/descriptions/tags, schedule uploads, and track publication history directly from the desk.
