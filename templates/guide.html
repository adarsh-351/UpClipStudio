How to Use AI Spark Studio

Follow these simple steps to transform any video into viral shorts.

Step 1 of 10
🔐

Sign In

Create a free account or sign in to access the Studio. Your session is preserved while you work.

📤

Upload Video

Drag and drop or browse to upload any video file. We support MP4, MOV, AVI, and more. Large files are supported with a friendly warning.

🧠

Video Analysis

AI analyzes your video to understand content, extract frames, and prepare for intelligent scene detection.

🎬

Scene Selection

AI automatically detects the best scenes and moments. Review the generated clips and select the ones you want to keep.

✂️

Optional Framing

Choose whether to apply smart reframing for aspect ratio changes. Skip it for faster processing if you prefer the original framing.

⚡

Generate Clips

AI generates perfect clips from your selected scenes. You can choose AI-decided clips or set your own duration and count.

💬

Animated Captions

Add stunning animated captions to your clips. Choose from multiple templates and animation styles to match your brand.

🎨

Customize

Fine-tune your captions with professional fonts, colors, positions, and animations. Preview changes in real-time before exporting.

👁️

Preview

Watch your final clips with captions burned in. Review everything before downloading to make sure it's perfect.

⬇️

Download

Download individual clips or grab everything as a ZIP. Export in your preferred quality and share instantly.

💬

Caption Studio

Open any video in the standalone Caption Studio editor. Add, edit, and time captions. Use search & replace, auto-split, and export SRT/VTT independently of video rendering.

📺

YouTube Desk

Upload your final videos directly to YouTube. Connect your Google account, edit metadata, schedule uploads, and track history — all from the YouTube Desk.
